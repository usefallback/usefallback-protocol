Mechanism

Four moves from sealed box to settled

  1. 01

    Select a sealed box

    Pick cards or timepieces, then settle with a single transaction from a Solana wallet.

  2. 02

    Reveal

    The item lands in the very wallet that paid, with its receipt one click away.

  3. 03

    Compare

    Fallback sets the cost against the item's appraised worth at the moment of reveal.

  4. 04

    Land softly

    Came out short? A share of the gap is sent automatically. Came out ahead? Nothing is owed and nothing is taken.

Cushion

One line of arithmetic

cushion = share × (cost − worth)

The share climbs with the size of your holding. Each threshold stays pending until it is stated, and the cushion never exceeds the gap itself.

Questions

Short answers

What exactly is refunded?

A share of the gap, but only for reveals that land below what was spent. Reveals that land higher trigger nothing.

Is there a contract yet?

The line at the very top of this page fills itself the moment an address is stated. Until then it reads pending and offers nothing to copy.

Where do the board numbers come from?

A public market index for Solana. They are shown as received and never edited. The board does not depend on the contract line in any way.

Is this advice?

No. Nothing here recommends buying, selling or holding anything.
