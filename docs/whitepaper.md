# Fallback Whitepaper

## Vision

Collecting should feel like exploring, not gambling against the clock. Fallback imagines a world where every sealed-box reveal has a floor under it, so curiosity is rewarded and a poor outcome is cushioned rather than punished.

## Mission

Give collectors on Solana a transparent, automatic cushion: when what comes out is worth less than what went in, part of the gap is returned in USDC, with every step checkable on-chain.
